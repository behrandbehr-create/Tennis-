How to use this site

Tap a topic. Everything you change here shows up for everyone in the league within a minute, no app or login needed.

For players

Find your nights and who you play with
Open Schedule. Use the All players menu to show only your nights. The tennis ball next to a name means that person brings a new can of balls. Tap Calendar on a match to add it to your phone, or use Download calendar at the top for the whole season.
Enter a score after your match
On the home page, played nights without a result appear under Waiting for a score. Tap Enter score, tap Played, type the games for each set (left number is Team A, listed first), and tap Save. You can also do this from the Schedule tab on any match. Ran out of time mid-set? Choose Unfinished instead and the match will not count in the standings.
Can't make your night? Swap in a sub
Text someone from the Sub list on the Players page. Then on the match, tap Change lineup and pick the sub in your spot. Everyone sees the new lineup right away.
Text, call or email anyone
Open Players. Every card has Text, Call and Email buttons. Text everyone starts a group text. On a match, Text the four messages just that night's players with the date and lineup already typed.
Put this on your home screen
iPhone: tap the share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen. It then opens like an app.
How standings work
Only matches marked Played count. Players are ranked by wins, then win percentage, then set difference, then game difference. Match tiebreaks count as a set, not as games.

For the captain or pro (Manage tab)

Add, remove or replace a player
Manage → Players. Edit names, numbers, phones and emails right in the list. Add to roster adds a player or sub. If someone drops out, tap Replace everywhere to put another player or sub into all of their remaining nights in one step.
Change a date, lineup or ball duty
Manage → Schedule & scores → Edit on the night. You can move the date and time, swap any of the four players, choose who brings balls, cancel the night or add a note like a court number.
Post an announcement
Manage → League info → Announcements. Type a message and tap Post. It appears on the home page for everyone.
Saving: how it works
Every change on the Manage tab saves the moment you make it and is shared with the whole league automatically. The green Live badge at the top confirms the connection. If you are offline, the change waits on your phone and is sent as soon as you are back online. The Save changes button at the bottom of Manage simply re-sends everything, in case you want to be sure.
Backups and starting a new season
Manage → Backup & tools. Download backup saves a copy of every player, match and score to a file. Import restores from one. Season generator builds a balanced rotation for a new season from any set of players.
