Starting a new league

For the racquet sports staff. About five minutes, start to finish.

Go here on any phone, tablet or computer(your league website address)
  1. Press "Start a new league"It is the green button under the list of leagues.
  2. Step 1: tell it about the leagueName, season, singles or doubles, and whether everyone plays the same night or picks their own day that week. Add your name, phone and email so players know who to ask.
  3. Step 2: type in the playersOne line each: name, phone, email. Press "+ Add a player" for more rows, and "+ Add a substitute" for the sub list. Phone and email are what make the Text, Call and Email buttons work, so put them in if you have them.
  4. Step 3: build the schedulePick the first date and how many weeks, list any holidays to skip, then press "Build the schedule". It evens out matches, mixes up partners and opponents, and shares ball duty. Look it over, then continue.
  5. Step 4: create itPress "Create my league". You get a link straight away.
  6. Send the link to your playersPress "Text it to everyone" and your phone opens a message with everyone already on it. Or press "Copy the link" and paste it into an email.

After that, you are done

Players do the rest

They enter their own scores after a match. Standings update by themselves.

Nothing to maintain

No files, no uploads, no passwords. Changes anyone makes show up for everyone within a minute.

Changing something later: open the league, tap Manage. You can add or drop a player, replace someone for the rest of the season, move a date, cancel a week, or post an announcement. It saves as you go.

Handy to know

Answers to the questions players ask most.

Someone drops out mid-season

Open the league, tap Manage, then Players. Find them and press Replace everywhere. Pick who takes their place and choose whether to change only the weeks still to come. Every remaining match is updated in one step.

A match gets rained out or moved

Manage, then Schedule & scores, then Edit on that week. Change the date, or set it to Canceled so it does not count in the standings.

Nobody entered a score

It stays on the front page under Waiting for a score with a green button, until someone fills it in. Nothing is lost.

Someone says the site looks out of date

Have them close and reopen it. If it still looks wrong, Manage, then Backup & tools, then Reset this phone. That reloads a fresh copy from the server.

Starting next season

Start a new league the same way, with the same players and new dates. Keep the old one for the record, or delete it under Manage, then Backup & tools.

Keeping a copy

Manage, then Backup & tools, then Download backup saves everything to the device. Worth doing once a month and before big roster changes.

A word about the captain code: if you set one when creating a league, only people who know it can use the Manage tab. Most leagues leave it blank so any player can enter a score after their match. You can add or remove it later under Manage, League info, Advanced settings.
