Your league, on your phone

Everything for the season in one link. Nothing to download.

Open this on your phone(your league link)

See your nights

Tap Schedule. Use the All players menu to show only your matches. The tennis ball marks who brings a new can.

Enter your score

On the front page your match appears under Waiting for a score. Tap Enter score, tap Played, type the games, tap Save.

Reach anyone

Tap Players. Every card has Text, Call and Email. On a match, Text the four messages just that night's group.

Need a sub?

Text someone from the Sub list, then tap Change lineup on your match and pick them. Everyone sees it right away.

Put it on your home screen

iPhone

Open the link in Safari, tap the share button at the bottom, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open the link in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then tap Add to Home screen.

Unfinished match? If you run out of time in the middle of a set, choose Unfinished instead of Played. It will not count in the standings. Got it wrong? Tap Edit score on the match and fix it. Nothing is locked.

How standings work

Only matches marked Played count. Players are ranked by wins, then win percentage, then set difference, then game difference. A match tiebreak counts as a set, not as games.

Stuck? There is a ? button at the top of every page with a short walkthrough.
