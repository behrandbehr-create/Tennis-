If something looks wrong

Five things to try, in order. One of them fixes almost everything.

  1. Close the page and open it againThe site checks for new scores when it opens. Nine times out of ten this is the whole fix.
  2. Check the badge at the top rightLive in green means it is connected and everything you do is shared. Offline means no signal: your change is saved on your phone and sends itself when you are back online. Nothing is lost.
  3. Reset this phoneManage, then Backup & tools, then Reset this device. It throws away the copy on that phone and pulls a fresh one from the server. Safe to do any time.
  4. Check you are on the right leagueEach league has its own link. If the name at the top is not your league, go back to the main page and pick yours from the list.
  5. Still wrong? Restore a backupManage, then Backup & tools, then Import backup, and pick your most recent backup file.

Common questions

"I entered a score and someone else's showed up"

Two people saved within a few seconds of each other. The site tells the second person and shows them the newest version so they can redo it. Just enter it again.

"The score I entered disappeared"

Check the match was marked Played, not Unfinished or Scheduled. Only Played matches count and show a score.

"I can't edit anything"

The league has a captain code. Ask whoever set it up for the code, or have them remove it under Manage, League info, Advanced settings.

"A player's phone number is wrong"

Manage, then Players. Type over it. It saves as you go.

Before you call anyone: open Manage, then Backup & tools, then Download backup. That saves a copy of every player, match and score, so nothing can be lost while sorting out the problem.
